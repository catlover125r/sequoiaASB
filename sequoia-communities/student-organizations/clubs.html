Sequoia prides itself on its 70+ clubs of unique communities and organizations. Clubs create spaces for students with shared passions and interests to be together. You can join as many clubs as want during your time at Sequoia. It's recommended that you're involved in a few clubs. If a club isn't represented at Sequoia, make one!
