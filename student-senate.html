Student Senate

The legislative governing body of Sequoia ASB — 77 seats, 50+ organizations, one voice.

About the Senate

How we govern

The Student Senate meets to discuss, vote on, and approve activities that have school-wide impact. Meetings are recorded and press releases are distributed to ensure every student can stay informed.
