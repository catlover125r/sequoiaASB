STUDENT SENATE #3

What is the Student Senate?

The Student Senate meets to discuss, vote, and approve activities that will have a school-wide impact. Sequoia Organizations will also be offered opportunities for support and promotion as a part of Sequoia ASB's commitment to creating positive experiences for all Sequoia Students. The Senate will be presided over by the Student Senate Chairperson (Ethan Politzer) and Secretary (Sam Richman) with advising from the ASB President (Emma Chiavegato) and Sequoia Principal Mr. Priest. The Senate currently consists of 77 seats representing over 50 Sequoia groups. Each meeting will be filmed and a press release will be posted (via @SequoiaStudentSenate) to remain accessible to the student body. As at all Student Senate meetings, there will be time for open discussion and to call for a vote in the following meeting, so please bring relevant questions or information to discuss. You may contact 813619@seq.org with any pressing matters. Your attendance is appreciated!
