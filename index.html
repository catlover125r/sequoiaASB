Sequoia High School's Associated Student Body (ASB) encompasses all almost two thousand students who attend Sequoia High School. Every single student enrolled in Sequoia is in Sequoia's ASB.

ASB's Leadership is committed to creating a unique, positive, and enjoyable experience for every Sequoia Student. The organization is run by students for students, and dictates much of Sequoia Student Life. ASB is led by its Executive Board and the Student Activities Director, Mr. Uhalde. The organization's members include: the appointed Executive Board, elected Class Officers, selected ASB Class Members, and all Student Body Members. Sequoia ASB operates alongside its partner organization, the Sequoia Student Senate. The Senate works directly with groups outside of ASB Leadership to more directly impact student experience and incorporate student feedback. It is our job to keep the Purple Reign, Unaliyi, Sequoia Spirit Alive!

Take charge of your Sequoia experience with Sequoia ASB!
